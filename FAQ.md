## Policy FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

We designed Lofi Riya to be simple, secure, and respectful of your privacy. Here are the most important points from our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

__Privacy and Data__

**__Q1__: What user data does Lofi Riya collect?**
__A__: Zero (0). Lofi Riya DOES NOT collect or store ANY Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from Discord users. This includes your User ID, username, email, IP address, or the content of your text messages.

**__Q2__: If you don't collect PII, what data do you store?**
__A__: We only store non-personal configuration data that is essential for the bot to function and remember your preferences. This includes your Server ID to save your custom music settings, volume levels, and audio filters, as well as specific Channel IDs and Role IDs for features like DJ roles or restricted music channels.

**__Q3__: What happens to my server's data when I kick Lofi Riya?**
__A__: When you remove Lofi Riya from your server, all associated server configuration data is instantly and permanently deleted from our database. We retain nothing.

__Terms of Use__

**__Q4__: What actions are strictly prohibited when using Lofi Riya?**
__A__: You must comply with all Discord policies (ToS/Community Guidelines). Specifically, you may not spam or abuse the bot's commands, exploit any bugs or glitches, or use the bot in any manner that disrupts its stability or performance for other users.

**__Q5__: What exactly are the Premium features?**
__A__: Premium membership unlocks advanced capabilities such as higher audio quality, exclusive music filters, increased queue limits, priority support, and additional premium playback features.

**__Q6__: What is the refund policy for Premium?**
__A__: If you request a refund before your subscription expires, the amount will be calculated based on the remaining unused duration, minus any processing fees. However, no refunds are issued if your Premium access is terminated due to bot abuse, command exploitation, or violating Discord’s Terms of Service.
